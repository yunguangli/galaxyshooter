Create a vertical Galaxy Shooter game with Python Flet module (you can use flet mcp for syntex) . Use as much as possible the  ./src/flet_game , a game engine written in Flet. 
Portrait mobile style  (400x700 or page.width x page.height properties in Flet, whichever larger).
 Player controls a cool blue futuristic spaceship (spaceship.png) at the bottom that moves left and right
  with arrow keys or on-screen touch buttons. The ship shoots lasers upward automatically or with tap/space. 
  Enemies (colorful alien ships, alient1.png and alien2.png and boss.png) come down from the top in formations. 
  Destroy enemies and avoid their shots. Add glowing effects, particle explosions, and starfield background that scrolls down. 
  Collect power-ups. Show score at the top, lives (hearts), and level. Include start screen and game over screen with restart. 
  Make it fun, smooth, and visually vibrant like classic vertical shooters and prefessional looking and levels. You can use some built in sound
  and visual effects in the flet_game engine or you can suggest where to downloand.  UI shall be centered. Make full use of all phone real estate.
  excessive use of update() can affect performance.

  assets: https://flet.dev/docs/cookbook/assets
  client storage: https://flet.dev/docs/cookbook/client-storage
  audio: https://flet.dev/docs/services/audio/
